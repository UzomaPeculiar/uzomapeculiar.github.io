Bank Loan

Developed an Excel dashboard for bank loan analysis, tracking KPIs (applications, funded/received amounts, interest rates, DTI) with MTD/MoM, good/bad loan breakdowns, and status grids in Summary view. Overview uses charts: line (monthly trends), filled maps (regional), donut (terms), bar (employment/purposes), tree maps (home ownership) for risk management insights.
